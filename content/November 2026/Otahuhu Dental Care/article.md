Dental practitioners recommend crowns when substantial tooth structure has been compromised beyond the capability of a standard filling. Crowns encase the entire tooth perimeter, evenly distributing biting forces and halting crack progression.

## The Crown Fabrication and Placement Procedure

A temporary crown shields the prepared tooth while the permanent restoration is being crafted. # Relieving Severe Tooth Pain Through Gentle Root Canal Therapy

Severe tooth pain can be debilitating, disrupting sleep, work, and everyday nutrition. When deep decay or physical trauma allows oral bacteria to enter the inner pulp chamber of a tooth, the living tissue becomes inflamed. Root canal treatment is a highly effective, gentle procedure that eliminates internal infection and saves the natural tooth from extraction.

Understanding what occurs during endodontic treatment helps dispel common misconceptions and reassures patients facing acute dental discomfort. Modern endodontics focuses on patient comfort, swift pain relief, and long-term tooth preservation.

## Recognizing the Warning Signs of Pulp Infection

An infected tooth pulp produces distinct symptoms that should never be ignored. Patients often experience lingering sensitivity to hot or cold temperatures, sharp throbbing pain when biting down, and tenderness in surrounding gum tissue. In advanced cases, localized swelling or a small bump may appear on the gum adjacent to the affected tooth root.

Seeking prompt [Root Canal South Auckland](https://www.otahuhudental.co.nz/services/root-canal-treatment) treatment stops the bacterial infection before it spreads into the surrounding jawbone. Modern digital diagnostics enable dentists to assess the internal anatomy of root canals with high precision. Timely intervention brings immediate relief from painful inflammation and restores oral stability.

## What to Expect During Root Canal Therapy

Modern root canal therapy is performed under local anaesthetic and feels very similar to having a routine filling placed. The dentist gently creates a small access opening in the crown of the tooth to reach the infected pulp chamber. Microscopic instruments are then used to carefully remove the inflamed nerve tissue and disinfect the canal walls.

After thorough cleansing, the canal space is sealed with a biocompatible material called gutta-percha to prevent future bacterial contamination. A temporary restoration is placed to seal the tooth until a permanent protective crown can be fitted. Most patients experience swift recovery and rapid relief from tooth discomfort.
