# Enhancing Lower Extremity Circulation with Medical Compression Garments

Prolonged standing, long travel times, and venous insufficiency can cause uncomfortable swelling, aching, and fatigue in the lower legs. Many individuals struggle with heavy legs because gravity causes blood and interstitial fluids to accumulate in the feet and calves. ## The Clinical Science Behind Graduated Compression

Veins in the lower extremities contain small one-way valves that prevent blood from flowing backward as it travels toward the heart. When these valves become compromised or muscle pump action decreases due to immobility, venous pressure rises and causes localized swelling. Graduated compression stockings exert the highest pressure at the ankle and gradually decrease pressure upward along the leg.
