## Article 4: Distinctive Roasting Traditions and Flavour Profiles of Indian Filter Coffee

The culture of morning coffee across southern India represents a cherished brewing ritual steeped in century-old plantation heritage. Preparing traditional [Indian Coffee](https://thefoodland.nz/grocery/coffee/) requires a slow gravity drip extraction using fine stainless steel filters and dark-roasted Arabica and Robusta beans. Blending freshly ground coffee with roasted chicory root imparts deep body, earthy bitterness, and a velvety crema that pairs harmoniously with boiled milk.

The ceremony of pouring coffee between two metal vessels cools the beverage to ideal drinking temperature while creating a frothy head. This traditional tumbler-and-davarah service provides an immersive sensory experience that delights both longtime coffee lovers and curious New Zealand coffee enthusiasts. High grade beans sourced from shade-grown mountain plantations retain subtle chocolate and spice undertones that endure through the entire cup.
