# Industrial Scrap Metal Recovery in the Automotive Sector

The automotive industry is among the primary drivers of global metal recycling and resource reclamation. A typical passenger vehicle comprises several thousand individual parts made of high-grade carbon steel, aluminium alloys, cast iron, and copper wiring. When vehicles reach the end of their operational utility, the scrap processing sector transforms these obsolete machines into refined raw materials for new manufacturing cycles.

Industrial automotive recycling preserves finite mineral resources, conserves immense amounts of electrical energy, and reduces greenhouse gas emissions compared to traditional primary ore extraction and smelting.

## The Mechanical Processing of Scrap Automobiles

Converting an intact motor car into reusable metal involves a rigorous, multi-stage industrial process. The decommissioning begins with depollution, where hazardous substances including fuel, lubricants, air conditioning refrigerants, and lead-acid batteries are safely extracted and neutralised under controlled workshop conditions.

The reclaimed steel and aluminium obtained from end-of-life automobiles are dispatched directly to foundries and steel mills for smelting into new structural products, building materials, and automotive components.
