## Key Offerings

1. **General & Preventive Dentistry:** Comprehensive dental checkups, digital X-rays, scaling & polishing, and tooth-coloured fillings.
2. **Emergency Dental & Extractions:** Fast same-day pain relief, broken tooth restoration, and wisdom teeth removal.
3. **Advanced Laser Dentistry:** Minimally invasive, gentle laser technology for soft tissue care and painless periodontal treatments.
4. **Restorative & Prosthodontic Care:** Custom porcelain crowns, dental bridges, and comfortable full & partial dentures.
5. **Cosmetic & Orthodontic Dentistry:** Professional teeth whitening and orthodontic alignment solutions.
6. **Community & Subsidized Care:** Free dental care for eligible adolescents (under 18), ACC accident treatment, WINZ-approved emergency quotes, and flexible finance (Afterpay, Q Card, humm).
