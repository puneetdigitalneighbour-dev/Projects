## Key Offerings

1. **Pantry Staples & Grains:** Authentic stoneground atta flours, aged basmati rice, lentils (dals), and specialty grains.
2. **Pure Desi Ghee & Cooking Oils:** Amul cow ghee, Kashish pure New Zealand ghee (up to 10L commercial tins), and mustard oil.
3. **Whole Spices & Regional Masalas:** Comprehensive spice selections (MDH, Shan, Everest, whole black cardamom, kasoori methi, hing).
4. **Bulk Dry Fruits & Nuts:** Economical bulk cashews, almonds, pistachios, prunes, and chia seeds.
5. **Ayurvedic Health & Superfoods:** Himalaya Wellness herbal supplements, Dabur remedies, makhana (fox nuts), and organic seeds.
6. **Fresh In-House Delights:** Famous hot Punjabi & jalapeño cheese samosas, traditional Indian sweets, and savoury namkeen.
7. **Nationwide Delivery:** Safe, reinforced courier shipping of pantry goods across both the North and South Islands of New Zealand.
