## Key Offerings

1. **Solid NZ Pine Bed Bases:** Handcrafted in New Zealand using sturdy local radiata pine timber, offering superior support, durability, and customized upholstery fabrics.
2. **Pocket Spring & Memory Foam Mattresses:** High-specification mattresses (SleepMax Essential, Posture Care Support, Care Plus X-Firm, Lucid Euro Top, CoreAlign, Pure Sleep Organic) providing targeted orthopaedic spinal support and motion isolation.
3. **Custom Upholstered Headboards:** Made-in-NZ designer padded headboards available in an array of modern fabrics, heights, and textures.
4. **Adjustable Electric Smart Beds:** Motorized bed bases (such as the Mlily iPerform series) offering head and foot articulation for ergonomic relaxation, improved circulation, and relief from snoring or reflux.
5. **Full Bedding Sizing Range:** Comprehensive sizing across Single, King Single, Double, Queen, King, Super King, and California King.
6. **Registered WINZ Quotes:** Official itemized quotation service with dedicated online checkout and expedited case manager billing.
7. **Nationwide Delivery & Financing:** Flexible payment terms (Afterpay, Laybuy, Latitude Financial) with fast delivery across Auckland and both North and South Islands.
