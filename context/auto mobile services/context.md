## Product Overview
**One-liner:** South Auckland’s trusted full-service automotive workshop and mobile mechanic in Papatoetoe, delivering certified Warrant of Fitness (WoF) inspections, precision mechanical & transmission repairs, auto electrical diagnostics, tyre/wheel care, and WINZ-approved quotes.  
**What it does:** Auto Mobile Services provides comprehensive automotive maintenance and mechanical repair solutions across Papatoetoe and South Auckland. Combining a fully equipped diagnostic workshop with on-site mobile mechanic capabilities, the team services Japanese, European, and American passenger vehicles, hybrid cars, SUVs, commercial vans, and light fleets. Core services include Warrant of Fitness (WoF) inspections and repairs, logbook car servicing, engine diagnostics and rebuilds, automatic and manual transmission servicing, auto electrical fault-finding, battery testing and replacement, brake overhauls, 3D laser wheel alignment, tyre fitting, and official Work and Income New Zealand (WINZ) quote processing.  
**Product category:** Automotive Repair Workshop, Mobile Mechanic Services, Warrant of Fitness (WoF) Inspection Centre, Auto Electrical & Tyre Care.  
**Product type:** Hybrid On-Site (Mobile) & Workshop Automotive Maintenance & Repair Service.  
**Business model:** Direct-to-consumer and B2B commercial fleet fee-for-service model (transparent parts and labor quotes); approved Work and Income New Zealand (WINZ) provider billing directly to Ministry of Social Development (MSD).  

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Small-to-medium trade businesses, courier and delivery operators, transport companies, and local commercial fleets throughout South Auckland and Greater Auckland requiring consistent vehicle uptime.  
**Decision-makers:** Private vehicle owners, suburban commuters, working parents, tradies (plumbers, electricians, builders with utes and cargo vans), fleet supervisors, and beneficiaries seeking WINZ-funded essential vehicle maintenance.  
**Primary use case:** Keeping daily commuter, family, and work vehicles road-legal, mechanically sound, and fuel-efficient without exorbitant dealership prices, unexpected repair bill shocks, or extended vehicle downtime.  
**Jobs to be done:**  
- "Inspect my car for its Warrant of Fitness (WoF), explain any defects honestly, and fix them cost-effectively so I stay road-legal."  
- "Accurately diagnose that engine warning light, knocking noise, or slipping transmission and repair it with quality parts without overcharging me."  
- "Come to my home or work site to replace my dead battery, flat tyre, or fix a breakdown when the vehicle cannot be driven safely."  
- "Provide an official, itemized WINZ quote quickly so Work and Income can approve funding for my urgent car repairs."  
**Use cases:**  
- *Annual / Bi-annual WoF Inspection & Remediation:* Vehicle is due or failed a WoF; needs fast inspection and transparent repairs (brake pads, steering bushes, tyres, rust treatment, lights).  
- *Logbook & Preventive Servicing:* Routine engine oil and filter change, spark plugs, fluid flush, multi-point safety check to prevent major engine failures.  
- *Sudden Breakdown / Mobile Emergency:* Alternator dies or starter fails at home or roadside; mobile mechanic dispatches on-site to get vehicle mobile.  
- *WINZ Assisted Repair:* Client requiring financial support from Work and Income for essential transportation maintenance, needing an accredited supplier quote.  
- *Driveline & Transmission Trouble:* Automatic gearbox shuddering, slipping, or delayed gear shifts requiring expert diagnostic scan and mechanical remediation.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Motorists in South Auckland regularly face inflated dealer repair bills, dishonest "pass-fail" WoF upsells, impersonal franchise service, and the acute headache of vehicles breaking down when they can't be safely driven to a workshop.  
**Why alternatives fall short:**  
- *Brand Dealerships:* Exorbitant labor rates ($160–$240+/hr), rigid booking delays spanning weeks, and an aggressive tendency to replace entire assemblies rather than repairing components.  
- *High-Volume Franchise Chains (VTNZ, AA Auto, Tyre Chains):* Impersonal, transactional service; lack the flexibility to troubleshoot unique mechanical issues; push standardized, high-margin part packages.  
- *Unregulated Backyard Tinkerers:* Lack advanced OBD-II scanning equipment, cannot issue official WoF certifications, lack insurance, and offer zero repair warranties.  
- *Standard Garages lacking Mobile Support:* Require the customer to organize and pay for independent towing when a vehicle fails to start.  
**What it costs them:** Hundreds of dollars in unnecessary parts and labor markups; days of lost productivity or wages due to lack of transport; stress from unroadworthy vehicles and police infringement fines for expired WoFs.  
**Emotional tension:** Vulnerability to being taken advantage of due to lack of technical automotive knowledge; anxiety over sudden budget-breaking bills; frustration with unreliable transportation.  

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Local independent workshops in Papatoetoe and South Auckland (e.g. Papatoetoe Auto Care, local mechanics along Great South Rd and East Tamaki Rd) — fall short due to lack of dedicated mobile dispatch, slower quote turnarounds, and inconsistent WINZ assistance.  
**Secondary:** National chains and dealership service departments (VTNZ, Bridgestone Select, Tony’s Tyre & Auto Care) — fall short because of higher pricing overheads, rigid corporate policies, lack of personalized family mechanic care, and zero on-site mobile assistance.  
**Indirect:** DIY driveway repairs and second-hand marketplace part swapping — fall short because of technical complexity, lack of specialist diagnostic software, safety risks, and inability to clear WoF defect notices legally.
