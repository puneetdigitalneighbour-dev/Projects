## Key Offerings

1. **Warrant of Fitness (WoF) Inspections & Repairs:** Certified vehicle safety inspections with honest, cost-effective remediation.
2. **Comprehensive Mechanical & Transmission Repairs:** Engine diagnostics, timing belts, head gaskets, and gearbox servicing.
3. **Auto Electrical & Battery Services:** Diagnostic scanning, alternator/starter repairs, and on-demand battery replacement.
4. **Tyres & 3D Wheel Alignment:** Premium tyre fitting, puncture repairs, and precision laser wheel alignment.
5. **Mobile Car Mechanic:** Convenient on-site vehicle breakdown response across South Auckland.
6. **WINZ-Approved Quotes:** Fast official quote processing for Work and Income New Zealand emergency financial assistance.
