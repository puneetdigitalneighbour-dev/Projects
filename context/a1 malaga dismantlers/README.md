## Key Offerings

1. **Top Cash for Cars:** Paying up to $12,000 for any car, ute, truck, van, SUV, or 4WD in any condition (scrap, accidental, unwanted, running or dead).
2. **100% Free Car Removals:** Free towing across the Perth metropolitan area and up to a 400 km radius in Western Australia with zero hidden fees.
3. **Eco-Friendly Auto Dismantling & Recycling:** Certified salvage recycling that diverts scrap metal and salvages quality reusable auto parts.
4. **Tested OEM Used Auto Parts:** Wide variety of affordable second-hand replacement parts for Japanese, European, and Australian makes and models.
