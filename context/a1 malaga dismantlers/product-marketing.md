## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Selling a damaged, old, or non-running vehicle through traditional channels is expensive, slow, and fraught with friction—while buying new OEM automotive parts is exorbitantly overpriced.  
**Why alternatives fall short:**  
- *Dealerships & Trade-ins:* Offer insultingly low valuations or outright refuse older, high-mileage, or mechanically damaged cars.  
- *Classifieds (Facebook Marketplace, Gumtree, Carsales):* Require roadworthy certificates, active registration, endless messaging with tire-kickers and lowballers, plus safety risks from strangers visiting home.  
- *Standard Towing / Impound Yards:* Charge hefty fees ($150–$350+) just to transport an undriveable vehicle to a dump.  
- *New Part Dealerships:* High retail markups and lengthy shipping backorders for older or discontinued car models.  
**What it costs them:** Hundreds of dollars in wasted towing and listing fees; weeks of lost time; yard clutter and property devaluations; continuous registration/insurance bills on dead cars.  
**Emotional tension:** Frustration over being lowballed, stress over property clutter, anxiety regarding vehicle safety/legal transfer, and feeling ripped off by exorbitant mechanic part estimates.  

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Local Perth wreckers and cash-for-cars operators (e.g., West Coast Car Removals, Perth Car Buyer, Malaga Wreckers) — fall short because of delayed response times, hidden deductions taken from quotes upon pickup, and restricted geographic coverage.  
**Secondary:** Private peer-to-peer marketplaces (Facebook Marketplace, Gumtree) — fall short due to flakes, time-wasters, roadworthy test requirements, registration paperwork complications, and security concerns.  
**Indirect:** Local municipal council car collection programs or metal recyclers — fall short because they often charge disposal fees, require owner drop-off, and offer zero or minimal monetary return.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Broken-down vehicle occupying valuable driveway space; mounting repair quotes exceeding car value; annoying council notices or warning stickers; frustrating private buyers ghosting after asking "is this available?".  
**Pull:** Receiving up to $12,000 cold hard cash today; prompt professional tow driver arriving at the door; clearing yard space effortlessly; zero towing costs.  
**Habit:** Postponing disposal ("I'll fix it one day"); letting the car sit in the garage or grass indefinitely; assuming scrap car removal is complicated or expensive.  
**Anxiety:** Fear of being lowballed when the tow truck arrives; concern about complex registration and plate handover forms; worrying that the removalist will damage the property.
