## Key Offerings & Services

1. **Modern Townhouse Developments:** High-spec, architecturally designed multi-unit townhouses featuring soaring ceilings, open-plan living, designer kitchens, and low-maintenance landscaped courtyards.
2. **Off-the-Plan Turnkey Purchases:** Fixed-price building contracts with staged deposits, providing price certainty and deposit security in independent solicitor trust accounts.
3. **High-Yield Investment Properties:** New-build residential properties engineered for steady 4%–5%+ gross rental yields, low vacancy, full Healthy Homes compliance, and new-build tax advantages.
4. **"Sell Your Land" Direct Acquisition:** Fair, no-obligation direct cash offers for Christchurch residential sections and subdividable properties with zero real estate agent commissions.
5. **Turnkey Development Management:** 5-phase structured build cycle from strategic land acquisition, architectural consenting, and weather-tight construction to pre-settlement inspections and warranty aftercare.
