## Key Offerings & Service Pillars

1. **WOF Checks & Compliance:** Warrant of Fitness inspections and seamless mechanical repairs to clear failure dockets.
2. **Hybrid Battery Repair & Reconditioning:** Specialist hybrid diagnostics, individual cell balancing and replacement, reconditioning (from $750), tested second-hand packs (from $600), and brand-new battery replacements.
3. **Car Servicing Packages:** Clear starting prices for standard petrol, hybrid, diesel, SUV, and commercial utes (basic from $120 + GST, full service from $200 + GST).
4. **Brake Service & Rotor Skimming:** Brake pad replacement (from $120 + GST) and precision on-site rotor skimming (from $150 + GST).
5. **Mechanical Repairs:** Comprehensive under-bonnet and drivetrain repairs including suspension, steering, clutch replacement, cam belt kits, and engine rebuilds.
6. **Tyres & Puncture Repair:** Puncture repairs (from $40), new tyres (from $85), wheel balancing ($25), and eco disposal.
7. **Auto Electrical & Diagnostic Scanning:** Computer diagnostic scans ($50 incl. GST), dashboard warning lights, alternator/starter repairs, and wiring fault-finding.
8. **Air Conditioning Service:** A/C gas regas/refill (from $150), leak detection tests (from $80), vacuum tests (from $95), and compressor oil servicing.
9. **Auxiliary 12V Batteries:** Free battery health testing, new 12V automotive batteries (from $180) with 3-year warranty.
10. **WINZ Vehicle Quotes:** 100% free written quotes for Work and Income emergency transport grant assistance.
