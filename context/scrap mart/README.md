## Key Offerings & Accepted Materials

1. **Copper & Brass:** Bright & shiny copper wire, copper pipe (No. 1 & No. 2), domestic copper, hot water cylinders, brass taps, valves, and swarf.
2. **Insulated Wiring & Cable:** PVC TPS cable, data/telecom cable, automotive wiring looms, and heavy industrial armoured cable.
3. **Aluminium Recycling:** Clean extrusion, window frames, cast aluminium, aluminium sheet, cans, radiators, and mag wheels.
4. **Lead-Acid Battery Recycling:** Car, truck, forklift, solar, gel, and AGM batteries (licensed EPA hazardous export compliance).
5. **Industrial HVAC & Mechanicals:** Air conditioning units (VRF, bulkhead, outdoor/indoor), heat pumps, compressors, and electric motors.
6. **Steel & Iron:** Roofing iron, car parts, brake rotors, and heavy structural steel with weighbridge capabilities.
7. **Commercial Collection & Bin Hire:** Tailored scrap bin drop-off and truck collection for trade, factory, and demolition volumes.
8. **Used Car Batteries for Sale:** Affordable, tested second-hand replacement car batteries available at the Auckland branch.
