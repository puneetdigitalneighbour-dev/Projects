## Product Overview
**One-liner:** New Zealand’s trusted scrap metal buyer, recycler, and exporter, delivering certified trade weighing, transparent weekly grading, and instant payment by cash or bank transfer across fully equipped yards in Onehunga (Auckland) and Hillsborough (Christchurch).  
**What it does:** Scrap Mart buys, grades, processes, recycles, and exports non-ferrous and ferrous scrap metals and lead-acid batteries from tradespeople, industrial factories, workshops, demolition contractors, vehicle dismantlers, and the general public across New Zealand. Operating full-service yards in Onehunga, Auckland (11 Edinburgh Street) and Hillsborough, Christchurch (48 Curries Road), Scrap Mart handles copper (bright & shiny, copper pipe, domestic copper, hot water cylinders), PVC insulated copper cables (TPS, data, armoured, wiring looms), brass (fittings, taps, valves, swarf), aluminium (clean sheet, extrusion, cans, mag wheels, radiators), industrial air-conditioning systems, heat pumps, lead, zinc, electric motors, compressors, steel, and lead-acid batteries (car, truck, forklift, solar, AGM, gel). Every load is weighed on certified digital platform scales and truck weighbridges. As an official NZAMR member and licensed second-hand dealer holding EPA hazardous export permits under the Basel Convention, Scrap Mart guarantees compliant, sustainable recycling and same-day instant payments.  
**Product category:** Scrap Metal Buyer, Metal Recycling Company, Battery Recycling Specialist, Metal Exporter.  
**Product type:** Dual-Location Physical Scrap Yard, Trade Weighbridge Facility & Commercial Metal Collection Service.  
**Business model:** Direct purchase of scrap metals and lead-acid batteries from tradies, businesses, and households; sorting, baling, and processing for domestic smelters and international export; commercial bin placement and transport for high-volume trade accounts; retail sales of tested second-hand car batteries (Auckland yard).

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** The scrap metal industry has historically suffered from predatory pricing, opaque grading where premium metals are deliberately downgraded, uncalibrated scales, delayed payments, and uncertified back-alley operators that dump hazardous wastes illegally.  
**Why alternatives fall short:**  
- *Unlicensed "Cash for Scrap" Mobile Pickups:* Lack second-hand dealer licenses, often use uncalibrated handheld hanging scales, offer lowball lump sums, and dump hazardous fractions illegally.  
- *Traditional Corporate Scrap Giants:* Slow, bureaucratic account-setup processes, impersonal service, long queues for small tradie drop-offs, and delayed monthly billing.  
- *Council Landfills & Transfer Stations:* Charge extortionate gate tipping fees ($250–$400/tonne) to dump scrap metal that could otherwise be turned into income.  
**What it costs them:** Thousands of dollars in lost scrap value annually, wasted hours in long yard queues, exposure to compliance fines from illegal waste handlers.  
**Emotional tension:** Skepticism and suspicion that yard operators are shaving weight off the scales or downgrading clean copper; frustration with messy, hostile yard environments.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| **"Why do you require photo ID to sell scrap metal?"** | New Zealand law under the Second-hand Dealers and Pawnbrokers Act 2004 strictly mandates that licensed scrap metal buyers record valid photo ID (NZ Driver Licence, Passport, or 18+ Card) for every transaction to combat metal theft. |
| **"How do I know I'm getting a fair grade on my copper or cable?"** | We practice open, transparent grading right in front of you. We explain the difference between Bright & Shiny, No. 1, No. 2, and PVC cable recovery percentages, so you understand exactly how your payout is calculated. |
| **"Do you pick up scrap metal from job sites or factories?"** | Yes. Commercial collection availability depends on volume, metal type, site access, and location. Contact our Auckland or Christchurch branch with photos and details to arrange bin placement or truck collection. |
| **"Why don't you accept lithium-ion or alkaline household batteries?"** | We specialize in lead-acid battery recycling (car, truck, forklift, solar, AGM, gel) under our EPA hazardous export permits. Lithium and alkaline batteries present major fire and chemical hazards in metal shredders and must be disposed of via specialized municipal hazardous waste depots. |
| **"Are published online prices guaranteed?"** | Published prices are indicative and reflect current weekly market conditions. The final price is confirmed at the yard based on actual weight, verified metal grade, and material cleanliness (free of excess plastic, rubber, dirt, or oil). |

**Anti-persona:** Individuals attempting to sell stolen copper/cable or refusing to present legitimate photo ID; customers attempting to dump hazardous waste (asbestos, gas bottles, biological waste, motor oil, lithium batteries).
