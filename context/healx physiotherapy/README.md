## Key Offerings

1. **Orthopaedic & Sports Physiotherapy:** Manual therapy, joint mobilization, and corrective exercise therapy.
2. **Medical Acupuncture & Dry Needling:** Pain management and neuro-muscular stimulation.
3. **Registered Massage Therapy (RMT):** Deep tissue, myofascial release, and therapeutic relaxation.
4. **Chiropractic Care:** Spinal adjustments, joint alignment, and postural rehabilitation.
5. **Specialized Clinical Modalities:** Shockwave therapy (ESWT), low-level laser therapy (LLLT), cupping, and pelvic floor physiotherapy.
6. **Rehabilitation Products:** Custom foot orthotics, custom knee braces, splints, and medical compression stockings.
7. **Accident & Work Injury Care:** Comprehensive MVA auto collision rehabilitation and WSIB claim management with direct insurer billing.
